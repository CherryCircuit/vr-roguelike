fix: spawn cone is a FIXED level front again (150 deg), never the head direction

Round-9 made spawning follow the live head direction. That was wrong: turning
your head redefined "in front", so enemies still arrived outside the level's
framing. Restored a fixed front axis:

- getSpawnPosition() uses getSpawnForward(): the level front captured ONCE at
  level start (captureLevelSpawnForward) and never the live view.
- Removed setPlayerForward() and the per-frame view ref so no placement path
  can read the head direction.
- Cone widened to 150 deg total (+/-75 deg). Vertical rules restored: bodies
  spawn at floor+1.1 (hard floor at floor+0.8) and the cone tilts up with the
  level's verticalAngle (10/20/30 deg) so late waves arc in from above.
- Boss/mortar/minion/leech/boss-spawn clamps and breach hazards all default to
  the same fixed axis; the training ground captures its own fixed front when
  training starts.
- Enemy visibility audit now measures against getSpawnForward() and includes
  phases where the head is turned 175/200 degrees away from the level front.
