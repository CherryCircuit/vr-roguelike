fix: restore historical spawn cone (world -Z, 100 deg, no camera)

Rounds 9/9b made spawning depend on the headset. It must not depend on head
position at all. Values were taken from commit 746eceb~1 (the last revision
before the alchemy bench landed), read directly from that tree:

- getSpawnPosition(): world -Z, 100 deg total (+/-50), origin at world origin,
  y=1.5 (air 0.5..3) + level verticalAngle lift (10/20/30). No camera reads.
  Only addition: hard floor clamp (body never below arena floor).
- captureLevelSpawnForward(): back to the historical constant
  DEFAULT_LEVEL_SPAWN_FORWARD - no camera read. Boss framing, mortars, minions,
  leech bursts and breach hazards all share that constant.
- training ground: same fixed +/-50 cone around world -Z.
- enemy visibility audit: head turned 0/80/175/-20/200 deg; every spawn still
  inside +/-55 of world -Z (max measured: 50 deg).
