feat: player-relative enemy spawns + readable scrolling menus

Enemy visibility (new test-enemy-visibility.cjs audit drove every fix):
- getSpawnPosition() and boss framing now follow the player's FACING instead
  of the world -Z axis, so turning in VR no longer spawns waves behind you
- boss minions, leech burst minions and breach hazards (flare, meteors, rift)
  are clamped to the front arc
- bombardier plants its body on the floor (was 30cm buried); the rift breach
  no longer pulls enemies down through the floor
- non-instanced enemy bodies use renderOrder 10 like the instanced pools, so
  biome overlays (sun reflection, horizon glow) cannot hide them
- updateEnemies skips holes left by nested destroyEnemy splices (frame crash)

Menus:
- ALCHEMY/EVOLUTIONS are tested before the card-selection cooldown, so they
  respond on the first press after a level change (was ~1.5s of dead buttons)
- evolutions + alchemy rebuilt as compact opaque panels above the floor, with
  scrolling windows instead of pages; label canvas width fixed (was ~6x too
  small and wrapped every alchemy label inside its button)
- cards/HUD hidden behind the panel, opaque quads instead of stacked
  translucent layers, no per-frame essence shader or counter traversal
- training menu redesigned: 5-row scrolling columns, in-label queue counts
  (dropped 21 counter textures), no rows drawn outside the panel
- new test-menus.cjs: first-press open, pairwise button overlap, panel bounds,
  scroll windows
